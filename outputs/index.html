Good questions

A few things to know.

This early catalogue is deliberately clear about what it knows and what still needs checking.

Does a flagged ingredient mean it is harmful?

No. A flag means there may be something worth checking for your situation. People can respond differently to the same ingredient, and formula and use matter.

What does the score measure?

The beta context score reflects the verified formula and Noit’s current documented flags. It is not a universal safety rating or a recommendation for every person.

Does Noit give medical advice?

No. Ingredient notes cannot diagnose an allergy or a skin condition. Ask a qualified health professional about a reaction or known sensitivity.

Noit is an early, source-aware beta. Product formulas can change, so every entry links to its source and entries without a complete ingredient list stay unscored. General ingredient guidance should be sourced from established bodies such as the FDA and AAD.
